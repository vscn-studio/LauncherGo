# LauncherGo 2.7.1-pre.3

## Web map: area markers

- Added an Area Markers layer and an administrator-only Add Area Marker context-menu action. Four display bands—4–6, 7–9, 10–11 and 12–13—switch area labels as the map zooms.
- Select, combine and erase rectangles at one-game-block precision, with undo/redo and mobile controls. Name and color are requested after drawing is complete. Existing areas in the same band are protected from overlap; different bands may overlap, and erasing does not alter other areas.
- Administrators can Ctrl-click to select areas across bands; selected areas remain visible while zooming. Merge selections into a new, higher-level area while preserving the originals and holes in their geometry. The manager includes name/band search and selection controls.
- Adjust border, fill and text transparency independently. Internal seams are removed and borders use a crisp, fixed screen-space width. Names are centered along the diagonal of the largest rectangle inside each area, with text sized to fit.
- Ordinary left-clicks no longer open area popups. Administrator Ctrl-click selection, right-click editing and the manager remain available. Areas support screenshot export, edit-conflict detection and hidden-region permission filtering.

## Web map: place images and interactions

- Place markers support adding, replacing and removing images. Administrators can enable the feature; it is off by default. Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP and BMP, up to 2560 × 2560 px and 5 MB. GIF and animated images are rejected.
- Only 480px / 1280px WebP thumbnails are retained; originals are not stored. Photo orientation is corrected and EXIF/GPS and other metadata are removed. Access follows marker visibility permissions.
- Images fill the detail popup's content width below coordinates and above descriptions, retaining their aspect ratio even without a description. Includes click-to-enlarge viewing and narrow-screen adaptation. Close and remove controls are background-free crosses; removal is positioned at the preview's top-right corner.
- Place, claim and measurement-endpoint labels now appear only at web-map zoom levels 13–15 to reduce distant overlap. Icons and claim areas are not affected by this text restriction.
- Players, Mounts, Spawn, Place Markers, Game Markers, Hidden Regions and My Routes are enabled by default; other layers are disabled. Login and permission restrictions still apply. Visibility preferences are saved per server address, survive page reloads and take precedence over the new defaults.
- Translocator icons and connections always share the same color and change color together on hover or selection, without thicker lines or icon rings. Double-click an icon to jump to and select its other endpoint.
- Route planning starts immediately; name and color are requested only when saving, and cancelling preserves the draft. Renamed the context-menu action to Add Place Marker. Editor rotation is limited to −60° through +60°, with out-of-range legacy angles reset to zero.

## Mount display and mounted teleport

- Added a web-map Mounts layer for entities currently ridden by online players, supporting vanilla elk, boats/rafts and VSAirshipmod airships. Real models and textures produce static, slightly oblique overhead snapshots with 16 directions and soft silhouette shadows. Small mounts are enlarged 2×; large mounts retain their size.
- Mounts track position and heading, shared vehicles appear only once, and dismounting removes them. Player-location and hidden-region permissions apply. Fixed curled animal limbs by generating a standing pose on a separate skeleton copy without affecting live game animations.
- Added the default-off Allow Players to Teleport with Mounts or Vehicles setting. Only the actual driver can initiate travel, moving the vehicle and seated occupants together. Boats/rafts require water at both ends; elk require ground at both ends. Airship drivers must be strictly above the highest destination surface block's Y coordinate and arrive at their pre-teleport Y + 5, subject to vehicle and occupant clearance checks.
- Drivers pay 2× the normal item cost and receive 2× the side effects; each ordinary passenger pays their own 1× cost and receives 1× effects. Existing administrator exemptions remain. Confirmation lists each player's costs and effects. An ineligible member blocks the entire party, with item, position and effect rollback on failure.

## Embedded mods and upgrade notes

- Updated ServerMap from 0.3.2 to **0.3.3**; the launcher version is **2.7.1-pre.3**. ServerAuth **1.1.0**, Gateway Redirect **1.2.0** and Server Bridge **2.1.0** remain unchanged.
- Stop the game server and map service before updating the launcher, ServerMap mod and web resources together. Update custom WebRoot files as well. No world saves or map caches need to be deleted.
- Mount-image capture requires at least one rider on the same vehicle to update their client ServerMap mod and reconnect. An older build displaying the same 0.3.3 version does not necessarily include these features. For this prerelease, verify elk, boat and airship appearance and multiplayer teleport behavior on complex terrain in-game.

# LauncherGo 2.7.1-pre.3

## New and improved

- Place markers support adding, replacing and removing images. Administrators can enable this feature; it is off by default. Supports JPEG, PNG, WebP and BMP, up to 2560 × 2560 px and 5 MB. GIF and animated images are rejected.
- Only 480px / 1280px WebP thumbnails are retained. Photo orientation is corrected and EXIF/GPS and other metadata are removed; originals are not stored. Image access follows marker visibility permissions.
- Images retain their aspect ratio and appear below coordinates and above descriptions, with click-to-enlarge viewing. Close and remove controls use background-free cross icons.
- Place images fill the detail popup's content width even without a description. Removed the 240px thumbnail cap while preserving aspect ratios, narrow-screen adaptation and background-free presentation.
- Translocator icons and connections change color together on hover or selection, without thicker lines or icon rings. Double-click an icon to jump to and select the other endpoint.
- Added a Mounts layer with a persistent visibility setting, showing only occupied mounts. Models use a fixed, 25° off-vertical view, a 16-direction static atlas, fixed lighting and soft silhouette shadows. Small mounts are enlarged 2×, large mounts retain their size, with a smooth transition between them. Models track position and heading, shared vehicles appear only once, and dismounting removes them; player-location and hidden-region permissions apply.
- Added the default-off "Allow players to teleport with mounts or vehicles" setting. Only the actual driver can initiate travel, moving the vehicle and its seated occupants together. Boats and rafts require water at both ends; elk require ground at both ends. Airship drivers must be strictly above the highest destination surface block's Y coordinate, arriving at their pre-teleport Y + 5, subject to vehicle and occupant clearance checks.
- Drivers pay 2× the normal item cost and receive 2× the side effects; each ordinary passenger pays their own 1× cost and receives 1× effects. Existing administrator exemptions remain. Confirmation shows each player's costs and effects. An ineligible member blocks the entire party, with item, position and effect rollback on failure.

## Fixes and adjustments

- Map layers, My Markers and My Routes retain their visibility settings after reloading the page.
- Route planning starts immediately. Name and color are requested only when saving; cancelling the save dialog preserves the draft.
- Renamed the context-menu action to "Add place marker". Editor rotation is limited to −60° through +60°; out-of-range legacy angles reset to zero.
- Fixed curled front limbs in elk and other animal snapshots by generating a fixed standing pose on a separate skeleton copy instead of freezing walk, run, jump or swim frames. Live game animations are unchanged; boats and airships retain their structural state.

## Embedded mods and upgrade

- Updated ServerMap to **0.3.3**. ServerAuth **1.1.0**, Gateway Redirect **1.2.0** and Server Bridge **2.1.0** remain unchanged.
- These additional changes keep ServerMap at **0.3.3** and the launcher at **2.7.1-pre.3**. Initial mount-image capture requires at least one rider on the vehicle to update their client mod to this build; clients and servers use the v2 directional-atlas channel.
- Stop the game server and map service before updating the launcher, ServerMap mod and web files together. Update custom WebRoot files as well. No world saves or map caches need to be deleted.
- Automated regressions have passed; complete in-game appearance and multi-player teleport synchronization for vanilla elk, boats and VSAirshipmod still need in-game acceptance testing. See [mount display](../server-map-mounts.md) and [mounted teleport](../server-map-mounted-teleport.md).
